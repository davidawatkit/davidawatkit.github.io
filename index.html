Case Study · IBM

From "where do I even look?" to an answer with sources

When something breaks in production, SREs lose hours digging through old incidents, change tickets and repos that never share an ID scheme. I designed the system that does that digging for them.

The problem

Incident and change records lived in separate tools. The same service, team or host showed up under a dozen spellings. Keyword search missed paraphrases, and vector search missed exact error codes.

My role

I ran discovery with SREs, translated their workflow into requirements, and architected the retrieval and entity-resolution layers, from prototype to a containerized deployment extended for an enterprise client.

Outcome

  • −30% manual research
  • −40% duplicate entities
  • 10,000+ records
  • Patent application filed
